How this gets built

Now Hero
  1. 0 to 10
    HeroThe soft glass cube bends the headline. The covers sweep in from the right as the text fades.
  2. 10 to 70
    RingFive covers on the inside of a big cylinder, camera inside it. The ring follows a smoothed scroll, slows near each project and gently settles on the nearest one when you stop. The object sits on the front cover’s corner, morphs per project and bends the cover behind it.
  3. 70 to 90
    LandCovers flatten and settle into the Work grid one by one. The object turns back into the soft cube and floats into the nav.
  4. after
    PageThe grid, then About with contact at the end. The docked cube is a home button.
Framer
One code component draws the ring and the object (three.js). It reads scroll progress from its sticky section with Framer Motion’s useScroll. Its five covers (image, title, link) are set in the component’s property controls.
Native Framer
Caption, rail, buttons, nav and the grid are normal layers. The caption learns the front project from a shared store override, and each cover links to its CMS page.
The forms
Drop, wing profile with airflow lines, signpost, ripples, ring with a pear stone, nest with an egg. Each keeps a drop somewhere in it. Smooth primitives with a soft blend.
Phones and reduced motion
No ring and no pinning. The object cycles through the forms in the hero (reduced motion holds the cube) and the five projects are a normal grid.
